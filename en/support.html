Do I need an account?

No. Heartroom works right after you download it and never creates an account.

Where are my entries stored?

Only on your iPhone by default. If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, entries are stored in your own private iCloud database. Only you can see them; we can't access them.

I can't unlock the app. What can I do?

The app lock uses your iPhone's own Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. We can't unlock it for you and there's no backup password. Please follow Apple's guidance for your device passcode.

How do I delete entries?

Swipe left on an entry in History, or tap the trash icon in the entry detail. Deleted entries can't be recovered. To delete data stored in iCloud, turn off sync in Settings first, then choose "Delete data in iCloud".

Can Heartroom replace therapy?

No. Heartroom is a private journal and doesn't provide diagnosis or treatment. If you're going through feelings that are hard to bear, please reach out to a professional or a local crisis line.
