Heartroom Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-25
Applies to: Heartroom (iOS app) and this website

Heartroom has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools. What you write stays on your iPhone by default. It's only stored in your own private iCloud database if you choose to turn on iCloud sync. We have no servers and can't see any of your entries.

1. Data we don't collect

Heartroom has no account system and no servers of its own. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or third-party tracking SDKs. We don't collect your name, email, device identifiers, location or usage, and we never sell or share any data.

2. Data the app handles on your device

The following data is created by you and used only to provide the app's features:

  • Entries: the feelings you choose, your main feeling, overall intensity, notes, and the time and time zone of each entry.
  • Reflections and reconnection notes: what you write when looking back, and what you'd like to say to your child.
  • Settings: relock timing, appearance, and whether iCloud sync is on.

This data is stored on your iPhone using iOS Complete Protection file encryption, so it can't be read while your iPhone is locked.

3. App lock and Face ID

Opening the app requires Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Authentication is handled by iOS; the app only learns whether it succeeded and never receives your face, fingerprint or passcode data.

4. iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

You can turn on iCloud sync in Settings to sync entries across your Apple devices. When it's on, all entries are stored through Apple's CloudKit in a private database in your own iCloud account, covered by Apple's privacy policy. The developer of Heartroom can't access this database.

  • When turning sync off, you can keep or remove the entries on this iPhone.
  • After sync is off, you can choose "Delete data in iCloud" in Settings.
  • You can also manage the app's iCloud data in the iPhone Settings app under your Apple Account > iCloud.

5. Retention and deletion

  • You can delete any entry in the app at any time. Deleted entries can't be recovered.
  • Deleting the app removes all entries on your iPhone.
  • If you used iCloud sync, delete the iCloud data as described in the previous section.

6. Contacting us and this website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or advertising tools. The only thing it remembers is your language choice (Chinese or English), stored in your browser and never sent anywhere. GitHub may process technical data needed to serve the site (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy policy.

If you email us, we receive the contents of your email and your address, and use them only to reply. You can ask us to delete our correspondence at any time.

7. Children's privacy

Heartroom is designed for parents. It isn't directed to children and doesn't knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or applicable law changes. The updated version will be published at the same URL with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email heartroom.app.support@gmail.com
